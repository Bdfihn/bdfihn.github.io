About

Hello! I'm Albert. I'm a Data Solutions Engineer at Insource Services, where I build data pipelines, AI-powered systems, and benchmarks for AI in education. I studied computer science and biochemistry & molecular biology at Boston University.

My research is about where language models go wrong when they teach — when a tutor should help a student and when it should hold back, and how vision models read, and misread, students' handwritten math. I do this work at Insource with collaborators at Ai2, MIT, Stanford, and UW, supported by CZI and the Gates Foundation.
